Frequently Asked Questions

Timeboxing is a productivity technique where you assign a fixed time period to each task. Instead of working from a to-do list, you schedule specific blocks of time for your priorities. It's used by some of the world's most productive people, including Elon Musk and Bill Gates.
The free version includes 3 daily priorities, 3 time blocks per day, 7 days of history, Brain Dump, Smart Notifications, and Dark Mode. It's everything you need to start planning your day with intention.
Premium unlocks unlimited priorities, unlimited time blocks, full history access, and cloud sync across all your devices. You can manage your subscription in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.
Open your device Settings, tap your Apple ID at the top, go to Subscriptions, and find MyDayfocus. From there you can change your plan or cancel. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
Cloud sync is a Premium feature. Sign in with your Apple or Google account, and your priorities, time blocks, and daily plans sync securely across all your devices. Your data is encrypted and stored on secure servers.
Yes. Your data is stored locally on your device by default. We never sell your data. Cloud sync is entirely optional — enable it only if you want cross-device access. Read our full Privacy Policy for details.
You can delete your account and all associated data through the app's Settings screen. This will permanently remove all your data from our servers. If you only use the app offline, your data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.
